# 008: A `load32` that wins under clang and gcc

Status: todo

`load32` is an `inline fn` because gcc doesn't inline the plain function, and inlined
it takes 21 to 29 percent off the small JSON bodies on a Ryzen 7 5800X3D. Under Apple
clang on an M1 Pro, which inlines the plain function itself, the inline form costs 3
to 15 percent at levels 6 and 9 ([decisions](../docs/decisions.md#settled),
2026-10-23). Find a form that is at least as fast as the faster of the two under each
compiler.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `bench/compare.sh` against the inline `load32`, on the bodies and Silesia at
      levels 1, 6, and 9, with gcc on x86-64 and Apple clang on the M1 Pro, recorded in
      `docs/results-history.md`
- [ ] The form kept is no slower than the inline `load32` with gcc, and no slower than
      the plain function with clang, beyond the runs' noise; or the task records why
      none is
